Transcranial random noise stimulation on excitation/inhibition balance in ADHD. NeuroImage: Clinical, 49, 103923.
Preliminary evidence suggests that transcranial random noise stimulation (tRNS) can modulate neural oscillations and improve cognitive performance in individuals with ADHD. This study investigated the effects of tRNS on the excitation/inhibition (E/I) balance in the visual cortex of ADHD patients using 200+ EEG sessions.

DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2025.103923 · Article

Contributed to systematic reviews, manuscripts, and grant proposals for neurostimulation-based psychiatric interventions.
