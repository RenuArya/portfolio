About

I'm a Cognitive Science Researcher with expertise in neural data analysis and computational modeling. I build EEG/fMRI signal processing pipelines and apply Bayesian Hierarchical Modeling and Drift Diffusion Models to behavioral data.

Currently working as a Research Associate at Marbles Health, investigating the neurological impacts of non-invasive brain stimulation (tDCS) across psychiatric disorders including ADHD, OCD, and Depression.

My work has been published in NeuroImage: Clinical (2025) and I've received a 1st Place Research Poster award at IIT Kanpur. I hold an MSc in Cognitive Science from IIT Delhi and a BA in Psychology from Kurukshetra University.

Off-hours: music, reading, and exploring the cognitive quirks that make us human. Always happy to chat about research, collaboration, or the latest paper — say hi.
