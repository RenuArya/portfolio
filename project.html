Fatigue & Cognitive Performance Independent

IIT Delhi Jan–Mar 2025

Longitudinal analysis of 20+ experimental sessions tracking arithmetic performance under fatigue. Demonstrated that structured rest breaks improved task performance (mean RT: 98.2s → 81.1s), providing empirical support for Cognitive Load Theory applications in sustained attention tasks.
