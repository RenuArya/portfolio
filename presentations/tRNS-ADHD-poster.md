# Research Poster: tRNS Treatment for ADHD

## Poster Details

- **Title**: Effects of transcranial random noise stimulation on excitation/inhibition balance in ADHD
- **Authors**: Dakwar-Kawar, O., Francis, A. J. A., Arya, R., Haribika, M., Roy, S., Mairon, N., Mishra, J., Berger, I., Kadosh, R. C., Balasubramani, P. P., Nahum, M.
- **Venue**: CGS Department, IIT Kanpur
- **Date**: 2024
- **Award**: 1st Position, Research Poster Presentation

## Abstract

Preliminary evidence suggests that transcranial random noise stimulation (tRNS) can modulate neural oscillations and improve cognitive performance in individuals with ADHD. This study investigated the effects of tRNS on the excitation/inhibition (E/I) balance in the visual cortex of ADHD patients.

## Methods

- **Participants**: ADHD cohort (N=20+)
- **Stimulation**: tRNS applied over visual cortex
- **EEG Analysis**: RSBL, FOOOF, Wavelet Transform
- **Behavioral Measures**: Cognitive performance under fatigue
- **Statistics**: Linear Mixed Models (LMM)

## Results

- Significant modulation of E/I balance following tRNS
- Improved performance on cognitive tasks
- Reduced fatigue effects during task performance
- Correlation between neural changes and behavioral improvements

## Conclusions

tRNS shows promising results as a non-invasive intervention for modulating neural excitability in ADHD. Further research with larger sample sizes is warranted.
